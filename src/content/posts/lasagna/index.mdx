## Ingredients

- 2 white onions
- 200&nbsp;g of ground beef / vegan alternative
- 700&nbsp;g of tomato sauce
- 3 cloves of garlic
- Salt, pepper, cumin, curcuma

- 50&nbsp;g of butter
- 50&nbsp;g of flour
- 600&nbsp;ml of milk

- 250&nbsp;g of lasagna sheets
- Parmesan cheese

## Steps

### Bolognese sauce

1. Cut the onions (2) into small dices and cook them in a pan on medium heat. At the beginning add some water.
   When the onions turn translucent, add some oil and continue cooking until the onions are soft and slightly caramelized.
2. Add the salt, pepper, cumin, curcuma to taste.
3. Add the ground beef / vegan alternative (200&nbsp;g) and cook to taste.
4. Add the tomato sauce (700&nbsp;g) and garlic and simmer on low heat for 30 minutes.
5. Add some wine (follow your heart for the quantity) and let the alcohol evaporate for another 10 minutes. I like to use Marsala wine for this.
6. Remove from the heat.

### Bechamel sauce

1. In a sauce pan, melt the butter (50&nbsp;g)
2. Add the flour (50&nbsp;g)
3. Mix until a roux is formed.
4. Slowly add the milk (600&nbsp;ml) while stirring and cook until it thickens.

### Assembling

1. Preheat the convection (aka fan-assisted) oven to 180&nbsp;°C.
2. In a baking dish, add a layer of bechamel sauce.
3. Add a layer of bolognese sauce.
4. Add a layer of lasagna sheets.
5. Repeat steps 1-3 until the dish is full or you run out of ingredients.
6. For the last layer, add a layer of bechamel sauce and parmesan cheese.
7. Bake at 180&nbsp;°C for 20 minutes.
